Bé vui học does not collect, store, or share personal information from users.

Bé vui học communicates with our servers through APIs to retrieve the content and data required to provide its functionality.

Bé vui học does not use analytics services such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services that collect personal information for analytics or advertising purposes.

Bé vui học does not request access to sensitive device information or permissions unless such access is necessary for a specific application feature.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, transfer, or share users' personal information with third parties because Bé vui học does not collect personal information.

Although the App does not collect or store personal information, we take reasonable measures to protect communications between the App and our servers.

Where applicable, communications between the App and our servers are protected using HTTPS/TLS.

Bé vui học is an educational application that may be used by children.

The App does not require children to create an account and does not knowingly collect, store, or share personal information from children.

We do not use children's information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking.
